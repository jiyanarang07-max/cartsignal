
# CartSignal — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product Overview
CartSignal is a checkout analytics and decision-support
tool for small Indian D2C e-commerce businesses.

## 2. Problem Statement
Small e-commerce brands may struggle to identify where
customers abandon checkout, experience payment failures,
or leave without completing purchases.

## 3. Target Users
- D2C startup founders
- E-commerce operations managers
- Product managers

## 4. Product Goal
Help businesses identify checkout friction and
prioritise improvements using data.

## 5. MVP Features
- Checkout conversion analytics
- Payment failure analysis
- Device-based performance comparison
- Estimated revenue at risk
- Evidence-based business recommendations

## 6. Key Metrics
- Checkout completion rate
- Payment-attempt failure rate
- Checkout abandonment rate
- Estimated cart value associated with failed checkouts

## 7. Success Criteria
- Correctly calculate metrics from test datasets
- Identify intentionally simulated friction patterns
- Display understandable business insights
- Produce recommendations linked to observed evidence

## 8. Limitations
- Uses synthetic data
- Does not establish real-world business impact
- Revenue at risk is not guaranteed recoverable revenue
- Recommendations require validation with real users
